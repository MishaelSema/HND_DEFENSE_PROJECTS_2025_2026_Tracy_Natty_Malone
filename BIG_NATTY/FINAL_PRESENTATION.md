## SLIDE 2: INTRODUCTION

**Background**
- ICT advancement has transformed how societies communicate and solve problems
- Community issues (road conditions, waste, water, electricity) are prevalent
- Traditional reporting methods are inefficient, slow, and lack tracking

**What is IssueSpot?**
- Web-based platform for reporting and tracking community issues
- Users submit issues with images and location details
- Administrators respond and track resolutions
- Community voting helps prioritize issues

---

## SLIDE 3: OBJECTIVES

**Main Objective:**

To design and implement a web-based community issue reporting platform that facilitates efficient communication between citizens and authorities.

**The system will:**
- Allow users to report issues with details and images
- Enable community voting to prioritize issues
- Provide administrators with management capabilities
- Track issue status from submission to resolution
