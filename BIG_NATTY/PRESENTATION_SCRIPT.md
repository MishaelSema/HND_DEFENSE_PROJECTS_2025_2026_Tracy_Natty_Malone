## SLIDE 2: INTRODUCTION (45 seconds)

"Background:
As you know, ICT has significantly transformed how societies communicate and solve problems. However, community issues like poor road conditions, waste mismanagement, water shortages, and electricity failures remain prevalent in many regions.

-traditional reporting methods are inefficient, slow, and lack proper tracking mechanisms.

**IssueSpot** is a web-based platform that allows users to report community issues with images and location details. Administrators can respond and track resolutions, while community voting helps prioritize urgent issues.

- Track issue status from submission to resolution
